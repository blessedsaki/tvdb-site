Themes

Eight themes — what kind of hardship each name is watching.

The eight themes — one level above the series

Method. When this scene names someone, it picks one attribute and lets it stand for the whole person. Different series pick different axes:
Species — 智人/神人/伪人 (reduced to “not one of us”); Circumstance — 挂壁/赌狗/刀工 (reduced to “a condition”); Region — 弗雷尔卓德/北欧 (reduced to “a place”); Desire — 星欲/道馆 (reduced to “a bodily urge”); Stance — 爱国/绿蛙 (reduced to “a political pose”); Scene — 抽象/果泥 (reduced to “their slang”).

This is why a name often means the opposite of the content: 享福 (enjoying blessings) = hardship; 大神 (great master) = an ironic self-label. Naming is itself an act of reduction, not description — so classification follows what hardship the name is watching, not what it says.

503 accounts are assigned to themes (one account can belong to several). The series layer (XX TV) is untouched — see Series.
Method：这个生态命名时，选人的某一种属性，用它代替这个人。不同系列选不同维度：
物种——智人/神人/伪人（降格成「非我族类」）；处境——挂壁/赌狗/刀工（降格成「一种境遇」）；地域——弗雷尔卓德/北欧（降格成「一个地方」）；欲望——星欲/道馆（降格成「一种生理冲动」）；立场——爱国/绿蛙（降格成「一个政治姿态」）；圈层——抽象/果泥（降格成「一伙人的黑话」）。

这解释了为什么名字和内容是反的：享福=受苦、大神=反讽自称——因为命名本身是降格动作，不是描述。所以分类不能按字面分，要按「它在围观什么苦」分。

共 503 个号已归主题（一个号可属多个）。系列（XX TV）那一层保留不动，见「系列」页。

Cognition & Speech (species-ification) 认知与言论（物种化围观） · axis: species

What it surfaces — Observing your own kind as if it were another species — “how can this person think that?”
Why separate — The largest branch (智人TV 57.96M + 神人TV 17.21M + 伪人/类人/直立人 views). The move is species-ification: never “why did he do this”, only “is this still human?”. It is the only naming system that reproduces itself — every term is a biology noun.
Original field note (Chinese)
把同类当成另一个物种来观察——「这个人怎么会这样想」。

体量最大的分支（智人TV 5,796 万 + 神人TV 1,721 万 + 伪人/类人/直立人）。手法是物种化：问题不问「他为什么这么做」，只问「这还算人吗」。民科TV（1,678 万）是同一逻辑的知识版——围观「谁有资格说话」。这是唯一一套能自我繁殖的命名：智人/神人/伪人/类人/直立人/人类，全是生物学名词，可以无限派生。

Labour & Poverty 劳动与贫困 · axis: circumstance

What it surfaces — Factory, delivery, day labour, rented rooms, sleeping rough — where a person sits in the economic structure.
Why separate — The foundation of the scene. The 三和 / 日结 / 挂壁 vocabulary all comes from the day-labour quarter around Shenzhen 三和; it is the only continuously documented manual-labour misery on the Chinese internet. “牛马” (beast of burden) and “韭菜” (leek) are self-deprecating labels the observed use themselves.
Original field note (Chinese)
厂、外卖、日结、出租屋、挂壁——人在经济结构里的位置。

这个生态的地基。三和/日结/挂壁这一套语汇全部来自深圳三和一带的零工群体，是十几年来唯一被持续记录的底层劳动现场。「牛马」「韭菜」是自我贬低的称谓——被观察者自己也在用。

Absurd Subculture & Pranks 抽象亚文化与整活 · axis: scene

What it surfaces — Scene slang, mean-spirited challenges, country-personification, freak creations — performance inside the group.
Why separate — Not entirely watching others: much of it is performance within the scene — 剑人TV's mean challenges, 果泥TV's country-personification (the “ch” fandom), 神车TV's modified cars, 抽象TV's 孙笑川 lineage. The hardest branch to bound: observation, or in-group entertainment?
Original field note (Chinese)
圈层黑话、缺德挑战、国家拟人、猎奇造物——共同体内部的表演。

这部分不完全是围观别人，很多是圈内互相表演：剑人TV 的缺德挑战、果泥TV 的国家拟人（ch 圈）、神车TV 的改装车、抽象TV 的孙笑川谱系。暗黑TV 是其中最暗的一支（事故/暴力现场，1 个号独占 79 条 1,074 万播放）。这一支最难判定边界：它到底是围观，还是圈内自娱。

Gambling & Addiction 赌与成瘾 · axis: desire

What it surfaces — Bets, stocks, loans, betel nut, alcohol, pills — the full chain of self-destruction.
Why separate — The most-watched branch (赌狗TV 23.11M) and the only one partly sanctioned by official discourse — reposters attach “anti-gambling” disclaimers. Stock and fund losses co-occur heavily with gambling in titles (387 / 134): retail investors' suffering gets filed under gambling, a classification peculiar to the Chinese internet.
Original field note (Chinese)
赌、炒、贷、槟榔、酒、药——自我毁灭的完整链条。

播放量最高的分支（赌狗TV 2,311 万）。它是这个生态里唯一被官方话语部分认可的题材：搬运号会挂「反赌」免责声明。说明道德风险最低、商业价值最高。注意「炒股 / 基金」和「赌」在同一批标题里高频同现（387 / 134 次），散户受难被当成赌狗的一种——这是中文互联网独有的分类。

Marriage & Family 婚恋与家庭 · axis: circumstance

What it surfaces — Bride price, pressure to marry, in-law conflict, child-rearing — economic extraction inside intimacy.
Why separate — 刀工 (a homophone of 力工, “labourer”) is the key: the labour-mindset theory defines save → buy → marry → children as the only purpose of a life, then stakes everything on one bride price. The surface is courtship; the core is transfer of labour value.
Original field note (Chinese)
彩礼、催婚、婆媳、育儿——亲密关系里的经济榨取。

刀工（力工）是这里的关键：力工思维论把「攒钱—买房—结婚—生子」定义为人生唯一目的，然后一次性梭哈进彩礼。所以刀工TV 的表面是婚恋，内核是劳动价值的转移。相亲TV 439 次提及、老婆 434 次，说明这是最日常、最普遍被围观的苦。

Sexual Repression 性压抑 · axis: desire

What it surfaces — Explaining all human behaviour as an unequal distribution of sexual resources.
Why separate — The only branch with a public theory — 峰哥亡命天涯's “sexual-repression theory”, the one of the three theories still actively spreading. 道馆 (homophone of 导管, “catheter”), 星欲 and 压抑 are the same thing written differently to dodge censorship.
Original field note (Chinese)
把人的一切行为归因于性资源的分配不均。

这个生态唯一有公开理论的分支——峰哥亡命天涯的「性压抑论」，是三大理论里唯一还在活跃传播的一个。道馆（导管谐音）、星欲、压抑三个系列其实是同一件事，只是规避审查的写法不同。

Regional Identity 地域身份 · axis: region

What it surfaces — Using cold place names for the Northeast — a local self-orientalism.
Why separate — Two uses coexist: coding — 弗雷尔卓德 (a frozen region in League of Legends) → the Northeast, and 北欧 → the Northeast, the same trick used twice; and literal — 江南 / 吴越 / 江浙 use real place names to watch local grotesque food. The observed take part themselves, which makes it self-mockery rather than outside condescension.
Original field note (Chinese)
用寒冷地名代指东北——地方性的自我东方化。

两种用法并存：①编码——弗雷尔卓德（LoL 极寒之地）→ 东北，北欧 → 东北，同一套逻辑用了两次；②直白——江南/吴越/江浙/东阳直接用真实地名，围观当地猎奇饮食（童子尿蛋、臭冬瓜、脚踩腌菜）。被观察的人自己也参与（网友地狱火 11.4 万粉，标题全是「XX这一块」），是自嘲不是外部俯视——这让它跟其他分支有性质差别。

Nationalist Irony 民族主义反讽 · axis: stance

What it surfaces — Patriotic performance turned into satire of extreme nationalism — the name means the opposite.
Why separate — “爱国TV之日本人太坏了”. Japan appears 643 times and the US 309 times in titles — the two most frequent words — yet this branch is not scolding foreign countries, it is watching the people who scold them, the same irony as 享福 = hardship. 绿蛙TV is the mirrored version aimed across the strait.
Original field note (Chinese)
用夸张的爱国表演，反过来讽刺极端民族主义——名字是反的。

「爱国TV之日本人太坏了」「打倒小日本」。标题里 日本出现 643 次、美国 309 次，是全部标题里最高频的两个词——但这个分支不是在骂外国，是在围观「骂外国的人」。和「享福=受苦」同一个反讽逻辑。「绿蛙TV」是同一手法的反向版本（针对对岸）。
