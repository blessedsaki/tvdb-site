Theme detail

Labour & Poverty 劳动与贫困

Axis of reduction — circumstance （处境）
What it surfaces — Factory, delivery, day labour, rented rooms, sleeping rough — where a person sits in the economic structure.
Why separate — The foundation of the scene. The 三和 / 日结 / 挂壁 vocabulary all comes from the day-labour quarter around Shenzhen 三和; it is the only continuously documented manual-labour misery on the Chinese internet. “牛马” (beast of burden) and “韭菜” (leek) are self-deprecating labels the observed use themselves.
Original field note (Chinese)
厂、外卖、日结、出租屋、挂壁——人在经济结构里的位置。

这个生态的地基。三和/日结/挂壁这一套语汇全部来自深圳三和一带的零工群体，是十几年来唯一被持续记录的底层劳动现场。「牛马」「韭菜」是自我贬低的称谓——被观察者自己也在用。
