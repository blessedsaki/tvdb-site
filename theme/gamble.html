Gambling & Addiction 赌与成瘾

Axis of reduction — desire （欲望）
What it surfaces — Bets, stocks, loans, betel nut, alcohol, pills — the full chain of self-destruction.
Why separate — The most-watched branch (赌狗TV 23.11M) and the only one partly sanctioned by official discourse — reposters attach “anti-gambling” disclaimers. Stock and fund losses co-occur heavily with gambling in titles (387 / 134): retail investors' suffering gets filed under gambling, a classification peculiar to the Chinese internet.
Original field note (Chinese)
赌、炒、贷、槟榔、酒、药——自我毁灭的完整链条。

播放量最高的分支（赌狗TV 2,311 万）。它是这个生态里唯一被官方话语部分认可的题材：搬运号会挂「反赌」免责声明。说明道德风险最低、商业价值最高。注意「炒股 / 基金」和「赌」在同一批标题里高频同现（387 / 134 次），散户受难被当成赌狗的一种——这是中文互联网独有的分类。
